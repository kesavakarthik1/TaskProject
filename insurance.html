GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about insurance?

What should I review before buying a policy?

Review what is covered and excluded, benefit limits, premiums, deductibles, waiting periods, renewals and claim steps. Confirm that the policy suits your circumstances with the insurer or licensed intermediary.

Does registering for early access buy an insurance policy?

No. Registration only records your interest in LifeTime updates. It does not purchase a policy, request a quote or provide insurance cover.

Will every claim be covered?

Claims are assessed under the policy terms and applicable requirements. Read the policy wording and contact the insurer for authoritative information about coverage and claims.
