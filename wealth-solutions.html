GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about financial planning?

Where should I start when planning my finances?

Start by understanding your income, expenses, existing obligations, priorities and timelines. Your circumstances are unique; consider qualified guidance for individual recommendations.

Is a wealth solution a guaranteed way to grow money?

No. Financial outcomes are never guaranteed. Investments and other financial products can involve risk, charges and changing values. Review product documents and terms carefully.

What happens when I register my interest?

Registration only records your interest in LifeTime updates. It does not provide financial advice, open an account or sign you up for a financial product.
