GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about investing?

Can I get guaranteed returns from investments?

Market-linked investments do not guarantee returns, and their value can rise or fall. Review the product documents and risks carefully before making an investment decision.

What information should I consider before investing?

Consider your goals, time horizon, financial situation, risk tolerance, liquidity needs, product costs and applicable taxes. Seek qualified advice if you are unsure.

Does registering with LifeTime place an investment?

No. Registration only records your interest in LifeTime updates. It does not open an investment account, place an order or create an investment relationship.
