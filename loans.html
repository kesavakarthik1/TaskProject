GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about loans?

What should I compare before choosing a loan?

Consider the annual interest rate, fees, total repayment, repayment schedule, prepayment conditions and any other lender terms. Read the lender's documents carefully before applying.

Does the EMI estimator show an approved offer?

No. It is a mathematical estimate based on the values you enter. It does not include every lender fee and is not an offer, quote, eligibility check or financial advice.

Will registering for early access apply for a loan?

No. Registration only records your interest in LifeTime updates. It does not submit a loan application or affect your credit profile.
